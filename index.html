Common questions

Do I need a MIDI keyboard?

No. You can play on the on-screen keys. A USB or Bluetooth MIDI keyboard or digital piano lets the app follow your real playing.

How do I connect a Bluetooth keyboard?

Open Settings in the app, tap Pair a Bluetooth Keyboard, then select your keyboard. USB keyboards connect automatically through a cable or adapter.

Which files can I import?

MIDI (.mid) and MusicXML (.musicxml, .mxl) files from Files, iCloud Drive or other apps.

Where are my songs stored?

On your device only. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no account.
