Piano SFG

Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Piano SFG is a piano practice app. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This policy explains the little data the app handles and where it goes.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app is stored only on your iPhone or iPad:

  • the songs you import (MIDI and MusicXML files) and any edits you make to them;
  • markings you write on scores;
  • your practice history, progress and goals;
  • your settings.

The developer never receives any of it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used only to connect a wireless MIDI keyboard or digital piano that you choose to pair.

MIDI keyboards

Notes from a connected keyboard are used on your device to follow your playing. They're never recorded beyond your practice results, and never sent anywhere.

Children

Piano SFG doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
